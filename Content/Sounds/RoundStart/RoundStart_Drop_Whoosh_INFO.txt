Round Start Drop Whoosh
=======================

Purpose: falling section at the beginning of a round
Length: 0.72 seconds
Format: 48 kHz, stereo, 16-bit PCM WAV
Contents: one short rounded air burst with a subtle low push

There is no descending whistle and no landing impact. Trigger this once at the
moment the fall begins, then play the landing sound separately on ground contact.

This is an original procedural sound created for this project on 2026-09-15.
It contains no third-party samples or recordings and may be used, edited, and
distributed as part of this game project without attribution.
